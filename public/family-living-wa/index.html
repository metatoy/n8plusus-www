Objective

A for-sale rural/wooded waterfront property in Washington that legally supports

THREE families in separate living spaces. Realistic target = one larger parcel

that legally allows MULTIPLE DWELLING UNITS (main house + permitted ADUs/DADUs,

Due-diligence checklist (before offer)

  • Confirm zoning + comprehensive-plan designation with Mason County planning (pre-purchase meeting).
  • Verify any guest house/ADU is a PERMITTED legal dwelling, not bootleg.
  • Water rights / well capacity legally supports multiple units; septic (rural = no public sewer; multi-unit may need community/engineered system w/ Dept. of Health approval).
  • Shoreline Management Act: saltwater/lake frontage likely needs a Shoreline permit; check the local Shoreline Master Program (setbacks, veg buffers, 200 ft shoreline zone).
  • Critical areas (wetlands, floodplain, salmon streams, steep slopes) = regulated buffers; SEPA review.
  • Title/survey/easement (recorded legal access if not on a public road), liens/encumbrances.
  • Engage a WA land-use attorney.
