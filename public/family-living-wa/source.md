# Family compound — WA rural waterfront search findings (2026-08-08)

## Objective
A for-sale rural/wooded waterfront property in Washington that legally supports
THREE families in separate living spaces. Realistic target = one larger parcel
that legally allows MULTIPLE DWELLING UNITS (main house + permitted ADUs/DADUs,
or an existing legal cabin/compound cluster on one deed) — avoids rural
multifamily zoning, which the Growth Management Act generally blocks.

Target counties: Jefferson, Clallam, Mason, San Juan, Skagit, Whatcom, Kitsap.
First county searched: **Mason** (wooded waterfront + existing cabin clusters).

## Due-diligence checklist (before offer)
- Confirm zoning + comprehensive-plan designation with Mason County planning (pre-purchase meeting).
- Verify any guest house/ADU is a PERMITTED legal dwelling, not bootleg.
- Water rights / well capacity legally supports multiple units; septic (rural = no public sewer;
  multi-unit may need community/engineered system w/ Dept. of Health approval).
- Shoreline Management Act: saltwater/lake frontage likely needs a Shoreline permit; check the
  local Shoreline Master Program (setbacks, veg buffers, 200 ft shoreline zone).
- Critical areas (wetlands, floodplain, salmon streams, steep slopes) = regulated buffers; SEPA review.
- Title/survey/easement (recorded legal access if not on a public road), liens/encumbrances.
- Engage a WA land-use attorney.
