About01

Nathan O'Brien is the founder of Metatoy, an independent product studio, and consults through N8+US as a fractional CTO and AI architect. Before going independent he spent 11 years at BuzzFeed as a Staff engineer — owner of its social-publishing frontend and the engineer who proved its first agentic-AI patterns in production. Before that, a decade of agency leadership: Director of Technology at Schematic on Target.com, growing OLSON's interactive team from 4 to 36, and early web work at Fallon and Martin·Williams — BMWFilms, Bahamas.com — back to 1995. Every era has been the same job, done at the newest seam between design and engineering. Today that seam is AI.
