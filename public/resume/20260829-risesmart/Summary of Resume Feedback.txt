Summary of Resume Feedback
General Formatting & Visuals
Enhance visual appeal with subtle color or shading to help the resume stand out.
Ensure consistent grammar, punctuation, and bullet styles throughout.
Fix OCR/encoding errors (e.g., "Al" vs "AI", "APls") and consider a single-column ATS-friendly version.
Contact Info & Header
Use a simplified header for subsequent pages that only displays contact details.
Headline Suggestion: STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER | AI-NATIVE & AGENTIC SYSTEMS | FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE.
Summary Section
Avoid personal pronouns; use a consistent, implied first-person voice.
Rewrite the summary to be more ATS-friendly, focusing on specialization, level, and key deliveries.
Skills Section
Keep technical skills at the top for technical roles.
Restructure skills into categories (AI, Frontend, Backend, etc.) for better parsing.
Incorporate modern industry keywords such as "Generative AI" and "Agentic Workflows."
Experience & Accomplishments
Surface high-impact metrics (e.g., 84% accuracy, 176% desktop improvement) in the first half of the resume.
Omit total years of service (e.g., "11 years") as dates already communicate tenure.
Show internal promotions as a "Growth path" or "Career progression."
Vary action verbs at the start of bullets to maintain engagement.
Earlier Career & Recognition
Compact the "Earlier Career" section to show progression without listing every project.
Create a standalone "Selected Recognition" section for prestigious awards like AIGA and D&AD.
