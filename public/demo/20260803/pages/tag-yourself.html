Overview

Shipped consumer feature: quiz results become personalized, generated share images. Won its A/B test decisively.

What I built

I built the redesigned share experience for quiz results: personalized, generated share cards produced by the image-template platform. Each card is personalized per quiz taker and built to earn the click before it reveals the result.

The share loop — result to personalized card to a friend tapping back in:

How it works

A finished quiz produces a personalized share image, server-rendered and composited, that a reader shares to a feed or a message thread. The link pulls a friend back into the quiz, which starts the loop again — the share-flow diagram above shows the shape.

Results & impact

The redesigned share experience won its A/B test.

  • +176% desktop share engagement
  • +82% mobile web
  • 59%→69% return via shares

Talking points

  • Why hiding the result behind a prompt earns the share click.
  • The tag-a-friend loop, and how it lifted returns through shared links from 59% to 69%.
  • Designing the share card as a personalized, generated artifact rather than a static image.
