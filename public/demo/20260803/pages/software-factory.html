How one engineer ships 4 products across 6 repos: spec-first planning, persona-cast agent pods, model-tiered fan-out, screenshot/sim verification skills, and a measured ledger — humans own every gate.

What I built

I built the operating model behind my independent work: an AI-native software factory that lets one engineer ship multiple products across separate repos. It runs on spec-first planning, persona-cast agent pods, model-tiered fan-out, and screenshot and simulation verification, with humans owning every gate.

How it works

Work starts as an approved spec, then agent pods execute it. Model-tiered fan-out routes cheaper models to bulk work and stronger models to the hard parts. Verification skills capture screenshots or run simulations to confirm a change before it lands, and a measured ledger tracks tokens and wall-time per pod. Humans own every gate. Results & impact

The model makes solo delivery across six active repos possible, with token spend and wall-time measured per pod. It is how I ship products in parallel without a team.

Talking points

  • Spec-first planning: nothing executes until an approved spec exists.
  • Model-tiered fan-out that routes bulk work to cheaper models and hard problems to stronger ones.
  • Measuring tokens and wall-time per pod, with a human gate at every step.
